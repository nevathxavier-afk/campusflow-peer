1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL FOUNDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CampusFlow Peer is an intelligent, local-first campus operating layer that 
connects scattered college information and converts it into actionable assistance.

KEY PRINCIPLE:
"Existing systems store information. CampusFlow Peer connects information to action."

KEY ARCHITECTURAL PILLARS:
1. Zero Preloaded Timetables (Strictly Nill):
   - Initial timetable state is completely clean and empty ([]).
   - Shows the typewritten "Schedule is Nill" upload dossier card.
   - 100% of courses, daily lectures, and attendance slots are derived dynamically 
     when the user uploads a timetable image/PDF.

2. Interactive Connected Workflow Graph:
   - Visualizes the complete academic data pipeline:
     [1. Timetable Document] -> [2. AI OCR Extraction] -> [3. Period Attendance Ledger] -> [4. Faculty Availability Grid] -> [5. OD & Routine Approvals]
   - Shows live data flowing between modules without confusing node graph physics.

3. Archival Paper & Typewriter Dossier Design System:
   - Warm Ivory Parchment (#FAF7F0) with authentic ruled paper gridlines.
   - Charcoal Typewriter Ribbon Ink (#1C1917) with Courier Prime, Special Elite & Newsreader.
   - Double-bordered rubber stamp seals (APPROVED, OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVE, ACTION REQUIRED).
   - One-click Paper Mode (📜) / Carbon Ink Mode (🖨️) toggle.

4. Four Demo User Roles:
   - Student (Nivedha)
   - Faculty (Dr. Arun Sundaram)
   - Department (CSE Office)
   - Administrator (Campus Pulse & "Why Are Students Asking This?" engine)

5. Auditable Deterministic Telemetry:
   - Attendance percentage, safe bunk buffer, and consecutive required classes.
   - What-If academic CGPA simulator.
   - Multi-tier routine approval routing with timestamps.
