1. THE PROBLEM PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In an institution, information is available everywhere:
- Timetable PDFs and images
- WhatsApp announcements
- Department notices
- Attendance records
- Faculty schedules
- Leave & OD approval slips

The information is not missing; it is scattered, disconnected, and creates friction.

2. THE CONNECTED CAMPUS PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCATTERED DOCUMENT INPUT:
   - Student or department uploads an image or PDF timetable.

2. DOCUMENT UNDERSTANDING:
   - Multimodal OCR segmenter identifies table cells, days, periods, course designations, rooms, and faculty.
   - Confidence scoring highlights uncertain fields for student review before commitment.

3. CAMPUS GRAPH CONNECTION:
   - Extracted periods automatically link to:
     * Daily Attendance Ledger: Generates Present/Absent slots for those exact periods.
     * Faculty Availability Grid: Shows when the assigned faculty is available in their cabin.
     * Campus Navigator: Connects room codes to walking routes.
     * Routine Approvals: Pre-routes OD attendance exemption requests directly to the faculty advisor.

4. PREDICTIVE ACTION & TELEMETRY:
   - Attendance percentage, bunk buffer, and consecutive required sessions recalculate deterministically.
   - Universal campus search answers student questions with transparent explainable reasons ("Why This Answer?").

3. DESIGN PARADIGM
